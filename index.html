Flagship Program · Cleveland Brothers

NAXT ERP Implementation

NAXT is Cleveland Brothers' Microsoft Dynamics 365-based ERP replacing a legacy dealer business system across Parts, Service, and Rental & Sales. This program spans change management, a structured UAT training pilot, process mapping, an LMS evaluation, and the technical documentation that ties it all together — developed alongside a cross-functional NAXT team.

Program Strategy & Change Management
The governance layer that made the rest of the program possible: clear ownership, a realistic model of how people actually adopt change, and a way to track progress week to week.
UAT Pilot Training Design
A three-day pilot with Parts and Service, built around one central instructional design decision: every process guide fades from fully-supported to independent across three deliberate versions.
Process Mapping & Support Model
Swimlane maps turning "who handles this" into an answerable question — for attendance tracking, SME escalation, and front-line support.
LMS Evaluation & Governance (LogicBay)
Co-led with Erica Knipple: a formal needs analysis (task-by-task, pass/fail tested against real use), a leadership decision log, and defined Campus Administrator and Trainer roles for ongoing ownership.

Program · Cleveland Brothers

ADP Workforce Manager Rollout

A phased training rollout for ADP's Workforce Manager — timecards, delegations, and dataviews — built around a curriculum crosswalk tracing every lesson to its source task guide, three co-facilitated workshops, and a from-scratch employee timeclock guide covering procedures ADP's own documentation didn't include.
